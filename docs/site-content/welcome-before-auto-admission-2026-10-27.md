# Welcome to MCMC Alumni Network / 欢迎来到满珍院友社区

A student-led space connecting current students and alumni of Moon Chun Memorial College, University of Macau. One useful answer, opportunity or introduction is a great place to start.

这是由同学发起的满珍在校生与毕业生交流平台。分享一条经验、回答一个问题，或简单介绍自己，就能为彼此带来帮助。

## Start here / 从这里开始

1. **[Ask Alumni / 向毕业生提问](/c/student-alumni-q-a/6)** — Tell us your background, what you have tried and one specific question. 写清背景、已尝试的方法和具体问题。
2. **[Share an Opportunity / 分享机会与资源](/c/opportunities-resources/9)** — Include the original source, eligibility and deadline. 请附原始来源、适用对象和截止日期。
3. **[Introduce Yourself / 自我介绍](/t/introduce-yourself/14)** — Share what you hope to learn or can help with. 说说你想了解什么，或愿意提供哪方面帮助。

Alumni: a short answer about your own experience can make a difference. Students: thoughtful questions help others learn too. English and Chinese are both welcome.

毕业生的一段真实经历、在校生的一个具体问题，都值得分享。欢迎使用中文或英文。

## Joining and your profile / 注册与个人资料

Register using a UM email ending in **@um.edu.mo** or a subdomain such as **@connect.um.edu.mo**. New accounts currently require administrator approval before first access; this is separate from routine posting. If you no longer have a working UM email, contact the project team through your existing college or alumni contacts before registering.

请使用上述澳大邮箱注册。新账号目前仍需管理员批准入站，这与日常发帖审核不同；没有可用澳大邮箱的毕业生，请先通过已有书院或校友联络渠道联系项目组。

New members fill in member status and their connection to MCMC. These are self-reported, not identity verification. Your MCMC-connection field is not shown on your public profile. Graduation year, programme/industry and ways you can help are optional profile details; fill them in only if you are comfortable making them public.

新注册者需填写成员身份和与满珍的关系，均为自填信息，不代表身份认证。“与满珍的关系”不显示在公开资料页；毕业年份、专业/行业及可提供帮助为选填，填写后会公开显示。

## Share thoughtfully / 分享前请留意

- Treat forum posts and public profiles as public. Do not post student IDs, private contact details, unredacted CVs or another person’s information without permission. 请勿公开学号、私人联系方式、未去除个人资料的简历或他人隐私。
- Share original sources and disclose any relationship to an opportunity. Check details independently and update expired listings. 分享机会请注明来源与相关利益关系，自行核实，并及时标注过期信息。
- Be respectful. No harassment, scams or spam. Use **Flag** to report a concern; trust levels do not certify alumni identity. 请友善交流，使用举报功能反馈问题；信任等级不代表校友认证。

Need help using the forum? Visit **[Site Feedback / 使用反馈](/c/site-feedback/2)**. We look forward to learning from one another.
