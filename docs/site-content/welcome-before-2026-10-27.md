# Welcome / 欢迎

Welcome to **MCMC Alumni Network**, a community connecting current students and alumni of Moon Chun Memorial College, University of Macau.

欢迎来到 **MCMC Alumni Network**。这里连接澳门大学满珍纪念书院的在校生与毕业生，促进经验、资源与机会分享。

## What you can do here / 你可以在这里

- Ask alumni about study, careers and life after graduation / 向毕业生请教学业、职业及毕业后的发展
- Share internships, jobs, events and useful resources / 分享实习、工作、活动及实用资源
- Find people with similar interests, industries or destinations / 认识相同行业、地区或兴趣的院友
- Give back by answering questions and mentoring younger members / 通过答疑与指导支持学弟学妹

## Start here / 从这里开始

1. Visit **Introductions** and briefly introduce yourself.
2. Browse **Student–Alumni Q&A**, **Careers & Internships** and **Further Study**.
3. Ask one specific question or share one useful experience.

## Community principles / 社区原则

Be respectful, protect personal information, and do not repost another member’s contact details without permission. Please verify opportunities independently and clearly disclose their source. Advertising, harassment and spam are not welcome.

请彼此尊重并保护个人资料；未经同意，不得转发其他成员的联系方式。机会信息请自行核实并注明来源。本社区不接受广告、骚扰或垃圾信息。

We hope every MCMC member can both receive help and help someone else. :sparkles:
